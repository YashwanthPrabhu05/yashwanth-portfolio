ZENOSmart EV Charging Management

As the founding product designer, I helped shape ZENO's EV charging experience from early ideas to launch. The challenge? Figuring out what drivers actually needed, cutting through the complexity, and making everyday charging feel effortless for 5,000+ registered users in Denmark.

From Zero to LaunchHelped shape the product from early exploration to developer handoff, making key UX decisions along the way.
Less Noise, More ClarityStructured onboarding, charging, and dashboard flows around what drivers actually needed to do.
Built to ScaleCreated a reusable, variable-based design system to keep the experience consistent as the product evolved.
The Four-Second RulePrioritized battery status and ready-by information so drivers could understand their charging at a glance.
